          What this costs

          Hosting, sync, and looking books up in the catalogues cost nothing — they run inside free allowances and would keep doing so with far more readers than this has. The one part with a bill attached is the recommender, which asks Claude and costs about ten cents a time. That's what the ten-a-day limit is for, and there's a ceiling of $20 a month across everyone; past it the recommender rests until the 1st and the rest of the app carries on. If you'd rather not share either limit, put your own key in above and your asks go on your bill.

          It earns nothing. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party scripts. Nothing here is sold, and nothing about what you read is for sale — the backend keeps one number per person per day and no titles at all. The borrow and buy links are plain links: no affiliate tag, so nobody gets a cut when you use them. If that ever changes, it will say so right at the link, before you tap it, and not in a page like this one.

          Your own key
